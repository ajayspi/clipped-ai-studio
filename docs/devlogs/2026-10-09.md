# Devlog: 2026-10-09 - The Epic 9-Phase Masterplan

Based on the analysis of over 20 state-of-the-art open-source filmmaking projects, we executed a 9-Phase Epic Masterplan to transform the studio from a linear prompt-to-video batch queue into a professional, real-time spatial video engine.
